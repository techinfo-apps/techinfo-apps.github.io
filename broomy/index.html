Broomy

A tidier Mac.
Nothing deleted until you say so.

Broomy finds what's safe to remove, uninstalls apps together with what they leave behind, and keeps an eye on your Mac. Everything goes to the Trash first.

Free · macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

One page per feature.
Swipe between them.

How much can go, and how your Mac is doing, on one page. Caches, logs, developer leftovers and what deleted apps left behind, each explained before anything moves. An app together with its caches, settings and data, each with its size, all to the Trash together. CPU per core, memory, temperatures and fans. How much your SSD has written, and how worn it thinks it is. Brightness and volume of your displays, and a straight answer when a monitor can't be adjusted.

Honest by design.

Trash first

Everything it removes goes to the Trash, so you can put it back until you empty it.

Never online

No network code at all. Nothing is collected or sent anywhere.

Hands off your drives

External drives are never touched, and serial numbers are never shown.

Says what it can't do

When a monitor or an output can't be adjusted, it tells you why and what to try.

Careful by default

Things worth a second look start unticked; anything git tracks or another app shares is left alone.

Free forever

No ads, no subscription, nothing to unlock.
