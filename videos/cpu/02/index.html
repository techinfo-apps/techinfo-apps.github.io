Why 6 + 3 = −7 in a Computer: Negative Numbers and Subtraction

Pressing play loads the video from YouTube.

Watch on YouTube Subscribe on YouTube

A circuit has no minus sign. So how does a computer store −3, and how does an adder subtract?
We try the obvious idea (a sign bit) and see why it fails, count backwards around a circle of sixteen numbers, meet two's complement, turn our adder into an adder and subtractor with four X-OR gates and one wire, and find out why 6 + 3 can come out as −7.

Every animation in this video is drawn by code, and the narration is an AI voice.
